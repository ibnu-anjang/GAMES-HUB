📖 Quoridor Rules

  • Goal: Reach the opposite outer row before your opponent does.
  • Moves: Walk 1 cell horizontally or vertically.
  • Jumps: Jump over opponent pawns. If the jump is blocked by a wall, you can jump diagonally.
  • Walls: Placing a wall blocks paths. Crucial: You cannot block a player's last remaining path to their goal.
